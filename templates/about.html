About Customer Segmentation

Customer Segmentation is the practice of dividing a company's customer base into distinct groups that reflect similarities among customers in each group. This platform utilizes Unsupervised Machine Learning to automatically categorize customers based on demographic and behavioral metrics.

Why Segment Customers?

Businesses use customer segmentation to design targeted marketing strategies, optimize product pricing, customize loyalty programs, and improve overall customer retention by delivering tailored experiences.

Model Evaluation & Selection

The project evaluates multiple clustering algorithms (including K-Means, Agglomerative, Hierarchical, and DBSCAN) using key evaluation metrics such as Silhouette Score, Davies-Bouldin Index, Calinski-Harabasz Index, and Inertia. Based on comprehensive analysis across these metrics, K-Means was selected as the best-performing algorithm for predictions.
